# Part 2: Business Metric Decomposition

## 1. The North Star Metric
**Metric:** Increase the percentage of users who purchase at least one item from their wishlist within 30 days of adding it.
**Constraint:** No monetary incentives (coupons, discounts, cashback).

## 2. Deconstructing the Wishlist-to-Purchase Funnel
A user converting on a wishlisted item within 30 days must complete a chain of specific behaviors:
1. **The Save:** The user adds an item to the wishlist (high intent).
2. **The Revisit:** The user returns to the wishlist page within 30 days.
3. **The Re-evaluation:** The user looks at the saved item and confirms it still meets their needs.
4. **The Trust Check (The Crucial Barrier):** The user evaluates if they are willing to risk ordering this item from Myntra *today*.
5. **The Commitment:** The user taps "Move to Bag".
6. **The Checkout:** The user completes the payment flow.

## 3. Necessary Product Outcomes
For the business metric to improve, we have to drive specific behavior changes:
* **Product Outcome 1 (Re-engagement):** Users must be organically drawn back to their wishlisted items within 30 days.
* **Product Outcome 2 (Trust Calibration):** Users must feel operationally safe to order. Anxiety around returns/refunds must be neutralized *before* they click Move to Bag.
* **Product Outcome 3 (Decision Finality):** Users need enough contextual information (size availability, fit confirmation) directly on the wishlist to make a decision.

## 4. Identifying the Highest-Potential Opportunities
Using data from the AI Discovery Engine (Part 1), we observed:
* **59.7% of barriers were driven by a "Trust Deficit".**
* **68.8% of our segment were "Trust-Gated Shoppers".**
* **Delivery complaints (54.3%) and Return anxiety (6.3%) dominated the qualitative feedback.**

Highest-potential opportunity areas to move the metric:

### Opportunity A: Contextual Trust Injections on the Wishlist Card (Highest Potential)
* **Insight:** Currently, a wishlist card just shows the product image and price. But our data shows users are paralyzed by return/refund anxiety. 
* **Solution Direction:** Surface personalized, dynamic operational guarantees directly on the wishlist card (e.g., "Hassle-free 2-day return in your area", "98% refund success rate for this brand"). This neutralizes the exact anxiety holding back the Trust-Gated Shopper.

### Opportunity B: The "Delivery-Assured" Wishlist Nudge
* **Insight:** Users postpone purchases due to delivery anxiety. They won't order if they don't trust the timeline.
* **Solution Direction:** Transform the wishlist from a passive list into an active logistics engine. Nudge the user based on delivery windows (e.g., "Order in the next 3 hours to guarantee delivery by Friday").

### Opportunity C: Closing the Visual/Fit Reality Gap
* **Insight:** YouTube data showed users leave the app to check "Myntra vs Reality" haul videos.
* **Solution Direction:** Surface UGC (User Generated Content), fit-tags, and real-life photos of the specific wishlisted items directly in the wishlist feed, preventing them from leaving the app.

## 5. Conclusion & Next Steps (Part 3)
Our metric decomposition shows that if we solve the **"Trust Check" step of the funnel (Opportunity A)**, we have the highest probability of moving the 30-day conversion metric. 

For **Part 3**, we will conduct user interviews specifically targeting the **"Trust-Gated Shopper"** segment to validate exactly what information they need on the wishlist screen to overcome their operational fears.
