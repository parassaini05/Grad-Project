# Part 4: Problem Definition

**The Problem:** High-intent shoppers use the Myntra Wishlist as a passive holding area rather than a stepping stone to checkout. They abandon items because the Wishlist fails to provide the pre-purchase validation necessary to overcome the fear of a bad product and a painful return process.

---

## 1. The Target User Segment
**The Validation-Seeking Shopper** (Gen Z & Millennials, Tier 1/2 Cities).
* **The Reality:** **53.3%** of our users leave items in their wishlist for weeks. 
* **The 'Why':** The wishlist currently functions as a passive "save for later" bookmark. It acts as a risk-mitigation buffer for users who have purchase intent but are paralyzed by pre-purchase anxiety.

## 2. The Product Outcome We Intend to Influence
**Increase the Wishlist-to-Bag Conversion Rate**
* **The 'Why':** Our Metric Tree (Part 2) identified the 30-day Wishlist-to-Purchase conversion is bottlenecked at the `Wishlist-to-Bag Rate`. 
* **The Constraint:** We must influence this conversion by eliminating evaluation friction without relying on price drops (monetary incentives).

## 3. The Root Cause Preventing Desired Behavior
To improve conversion, we must address the **Pre-Purchase Validation Deficit**, composed of two deep-seated anxieties causing users to hesitate:

1. **The Expectation vs. Reality Gap (Fit & Fabric Paranoia):** 
   * *The 'Why':* Users fundamentally mistrust professional product photography. They hesitate because they cannot physically touch the fabric or see how the item drapes on a "normal" body type similar to theirs.
   * *The Data:* **46.7%** explicitly cite: *"I liked it, but I wasn't sure if it would look good on my body type"* as the primary reason they delay a purchase.

2. **The Operational Trust Deficit (Returns & Refunds Fear):** 
   * *The 'Why':* Users are terrified of the "reverse logistics" hassle. If a product doesn't look as expected, the burden of returning it falls entirely on them. This perceived risk kills checkout momentum.
   * *The Data:* **46.7%** rate their anxiety around incorrect sizing and return hassles as High. When asked what builds confidence without a discount, a combined **73.3%** ranked "Hassle-Free Return Guarantee" or "Return Success Metrics" as their top choice.

## 4. Existing User Workarounds
Because the Wishlist provides no real-time validation, it forces users to hunt for truth elsewhere.
* **Why do they exit Myntra?** They desperately seek raw, unfiltered proof before committing. They leave the app to search YouTube for try-on hauls, check Amazon/AJIO for realistic customer photos, and ask friends/Reddit for unbiased quality verification.

## 5. Why Solving the Problem Creates Meaningful User Value
* **The 'Why':** By solving the Validation Deficit directly inside the Wishlist, we eliminate the emotional anxiety and cognitive load of cross-platform research. We empower users to make confident, immediate decisions by proactively answering *"Will this look good on me?"* and *"What happens if it doesn't?"* without leaving the app.

## 6. Why Solving the Problem Makes Business Sense
* **Unlocking High-Intent Demand:** Items in a wishlist represent guaranteed demand. Unblocking this is substantially more cost-effective (lower CAC) than running performance marketing for new users.
* **Reducing Reverse Logistics:** Forcing validation *before* checkout significantly reduces expensive return shipping and processing costs.
* **Plugging Ecosystem Leaks:** Preventing users from leaving Myntra to validate products elsewhere ensures the platform doesn't lose the sale to competitors like AJIO or Amazon.

---

## 7. Evolution of Thinking

Our research systematically guided the problem statement from assumption to evidence:

1. **Business Metric:** Increase the 30-day Wishlist-to-Purchase conversion rate.
2. **Product Outcomes:** Decomposing the metric revealed that `Wishlist-to-Bag Rate` is the true bottleneck; intent must increase without relying on price drops.
3. **AI Discovery:** Analyzing 9,100+ raw data points via an LLM highlighted a massive, unexpected variable: **Operational Trust**. The AI generated three core hypotheses:
   * *Hypothesis 1:* The primary barrier to wishlist conversion isn't price or product, but a **Trust Deficit** (identified in 59.7% of signals).
   * *Hypothesis 2:* Users use the wishlist as a "holding area" to delay risk because they suffer from **Delivery Anxiety and Return Friction**.
   * *Hypothesis 3:* The dominant segment is the **"Trust-Gated Shopper"** (68.8% of users) who has intent to buy but is paralyzed by platform unreliability.
4. **Primary Research:** Our survey and qualitative feedback confirmed the AI's hypotheses. While we initially assumed Fit Predictors were the ultimate solution, 40% of users ranked a simple "Hassle-Free Return Guarantee" as the highest driver of purchasing confidence, perfectly validating the AI's discovery.
5. **Problem Definition:** 
   * *Initial Assumption:* Users abandon wishlists primarily because of price or because they don't know their size.
   * *Final Evidence-Backed Definition:* Wishlist conversion is constrained by a lack of operational and product validation. Users hold items because they suffer from high anxiety regarding fit/fabric (46.7%) and fundamentally require upfront guarantees that the return process won't trap their money (73.3% combined demand for operational assurances).
