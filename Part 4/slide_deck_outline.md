# 10-Slide Presentation Flow: Myntra Wishlist Conversion

Based on the requirements in `Problem.txt`, here is a strategic 10-slide flow. 

> **Slide Titles:** As per the guidelines, slide titles MUST state the key message of the slide rather than a generic section name (e.g., use "Fit Uncertainty is the Root Cause of Wishlist Abandonment" instead of "Problem Definition").

---

## Slide 1: Title Slide (or Executive Summary)
- **Title:** Unlocking Myntra Wishlist Conversions Through AI-Powered Product Validation
- **Content:** Briefly introduce the goal (increasing 30-day wishlist-to-purchase conversion without discounts).

## Slide 2: Business Metric Decomposition (Part 2)
- **Title:** Converting Wishlists Requires Solving Pre-Purchase Friction, Not Just Lowering Prices
- **Content:** Show your metric tree. Break down Wishlist → Purchase conversion into product outcomes (e.g., increasing intent-to-buy vs. decreasing checkout drop-off). Highlight that solving "Confidence" is the highest-leverage lever that doesn't involve discounting.

## Slide 3: AI Discovery Engine Architecture & Workflow (Part 1)
- **Title:** Groq-Powered AI Engine Analyzed 9,000+ Reviews to Uncover Hidden Conversion Barriers
- **Content:** This fulfills the requirement for *"A 1-slider within the final deck explaining how it works"*. Briefly show the architecture (Data sources → LLM Processing → Insights) and include the **Link to the AI Discovery Engine**.

## Slide 4: AI Discovery Findings (Part 1)
- **Title:** Discovery Data Reveals a Massive Trust Deficit and Expectation Gap
- **Content:** Present the key insights from your AI analysis. Show that users use the wishlist as a "holding area" because they are anxious about operational reliability (returns/refunds) and the visual reality gap between catalog photos and actual products.

## Slide 5: Primary Research & Validation (Part 3)
- **Title:** Survey & Interviews Confirm: Users Need Fit & Quality Proof Before Checking Out
- **Content:** Highlight findings from your 5 interviews and survey. Share a compelling user quote. Show that users actively leave Myntra (to check YouTube hauls or Reddit) to validate if a product will actually fit their body type and if the fabric is good.

## Slide 6: Problem Definition (Part 4)
- **Title:** Wishlist Conversion is Constrained by a Lack of Personalized, Real-World Validation
- **Content:** Synthesize the research into the core problem: "The Expectation vs Reality Gap". Define the target segment (*The Hesitant High-Intent Shopper*) and explain that without real-world proof of fit and quality, they postpone the purchase or abandon it entirely. 

## Slide 7: Solution Rationale (Part 5 Context)
- **Title:** We Must Provide 'Real-World Proof' Directly Inside the Wishlist Ecosystem
- **Content:** Explain *why* your chosen MVP solves the root cause. If the problem is that users leave the app to find validation, the solution must bring hyper-personalized, trustworthy validation directly into the Myntra interface (without using discounts).

## Slide 8: The MVP Showcase (Part 5)
- **Title:** Introducing [MVP Name]: Validating Fit and Fabric Without Leaving Myntra
- **Content:** Show the MVP design/interface. This could be an AI agent, a new feature, or a connected workflow (e.g., an AI that aggregates real customer photos matching the user's body type). Include the **Link to the Deployed MVP**.

## Slide 9: Defining Success Metrics (Part 6)
- **Title:** Measuring Success Through Increased Conversion and Reduced Return Rates
- **Content:** 
  - *North Star:* % increase in 30-day wishlist-to-bag conversion.
  - *Leading Indicators:* MVP feature engagement rate, reduction in time spent in wishlist.
  - *Guardrail Metric:* Return rate (ensuring we aren't just driving bad conversions).

## Slide 10: Risks & Mitigation (Part 7)
- **Title:** Mitigating Risks Around Data Privacy and AI Hallucinations
- **Content:** Identify the biggest risks of your MVP (e.g., AI generating incorrect fit advice leading to higher returns, or users ignoring the new feature). Provide clear mitigation steps for each.
