⚠️ PM Prioritisation — Why Delivery for MVP, Not Return?
The updated survey (n=20) shows Delivery Anxiety as the #1 fear, perfectly aligning with the AI engine (n=221) which shows Delivery Complaints at 54.3% of all evidence.
Delivery anxiety is a pre-checkout blocker (kills the 'Move to Bag' click before it happens). Delivery anxiety is post-checkout — only relevant after conversion.
Decision: Solve the pre-purchase blocker first → "Need it By?" delivery selector = MVP. Return Guarantee = Phase 2.
Key Validation 85.0% of users seek trust signals outside Myntra. While Return features are highly desired, Survey (Q9) quantitative data proves Delivery Anxiety is the highest rated fear. Delivery chosen as the MVP focus. Return Guarantee → Phase 2
