Success Metrics
Evaluating the "Need it By?" Date Selector MVP
🎯
Primary Business Metric (The North Star)
Conversion Rate & Incremental GMV
Formula: (Orders / Wishlist Sessions) × 100 & Δ Baseline GMV
📈
Leading Indicators
1. "Need it by?" Engagement
(Sessions with click / Total sessions) * 100
Measures feature discoverability and initial user curiosity.
2. Date-Locked to Bag Conv.
(Items moved to bag post-selection / Total items with selected date) * 100
Core hypothesis validation — a guaranteed date must drive a significantly higher move-to-bag rate than the baseline.
3. Time-to-Bag
Σ(Time bag - Time added) / Total moved to bag
Expected to accelerate decisions and reduce idle time in the wishlist.
🛡️
Guardrail Metrics
1. Post-Date Lock Bag Abandon.
(Abandoned date-locked items / Total date-locked items moved to bag) * 100
Ensures we aren't generating false intent via the 15-minute lock.
2. Wishlist Addition Rate
Total items added / Total active sessions
Ensures the new calendar UI doesn't make the wishlist feel cluttered.
3. AOV Impact
AOV (Standard Checkouts) - AOV (Date-Locked)
The per-item calendar may fragment baskets into single-item checkouts, lowering AOV and hurting unit economics.
🚚
Operational Metrics
1. On-Time Delivery Rate
(Date-locked orders on time / Total date-locked orders) * 100
Tracks "The Broken Promise". Failing to meet a guaranteed date causes permanent churn.
2. Date Selection Completion
Confirmed selections / Calendar opens
Catches friction inside the calendar UX — if users open it but don't confirm, the dates shown or the interaction itself is broken.
3. Timer Completion Rate
Bags in 15 mins / Total date-locked items
Measures if the 15-min micro-commitment is working or if the window is too short / urgency too weak.
Strategic Validation
Success is defined by eliminating operational anxiety and driving active wishlist conversion.
