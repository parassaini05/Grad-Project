The Trust-Gated Shopper
Primary Target Segment
Age 25–34
Occupation Working Professional
Myntra Usage 1–2× / month
Primary Goal Occasion-specific apparel
Behaviour
Adds items to wishlist, leaves for weeks without buying.
Checks Amazon / Ajio for faster or guaranteed delivery.
Seeks external trust via YouTube hauls and Instagram reels.
Pain Points
Wishlist cards hide exact delivery dates until checkout.
Hard event deadlines (weddings, travel) make vague windows a risk.
Fear of complex returns if product doesn't match the photo.
Job To Be Done
When I find an outfit for an upcoming occasion,
I want to know the exact delivery date upfront,
So I can buy it confidently without fear of it arriving too late.
Customer Journey — Where Conversion Fails

🤔 Wishlist (Evaluate)

💥 Delivery Anxiety — BLOCKER
"Can't risk it arriving late. Checking Amazon."
✅ Purchase
"I know exactly when this arrives."

The Core Problem Statement
54.3% of high-intent wishlist users defer or abandon purchases because exact delivery timelines are hidden until checkout — creating a fatal pre-purchase delivery anxiety that blocks conversions and drives 40% of users to switch to competitors for guaranteed dates.
🎯 THE DESIGN QUESTION
How might we help high-intent Myntra wishlist users confidently commit to a purchase by surfacing delivery certainty at the moment of evaluation — before they reach checkout?
