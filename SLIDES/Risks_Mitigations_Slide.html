Risk 01 · Logistics
The "Broken Promise"
Logistics Failure
⚠ High Impact
Why it might fail
A supply chain delay breaks our guaranteed promise. For the 68.8% of users who are "Trust-Gated", this validates their fears and triggers permanent churn.
Guardrail
On-Time Delivery Rate
Mitigation Plan
1
Algorithmic PaddingOnly offer dates with a >98% historical success rate for that specific route.
2
Proactive RecoveryIf delay detected, trigger pre-emptive WhatsApp apology + ₹500 Myntra cash.
Risk 02 · UX Design
UI Clutter &
Cognitive Overload
◆ Medium Impact
Why it might fail
Adding a calendar to every card could overwhelm the clean UI. This risks dropping engagement from the 53.3% of users who use Wishlist for casual browsing.
Guardrail
Wishlist Addition Rate
Mitigation Plan
1
Progressive DisclosureHide the full calendar by default. Only show a subtle "Need it by?" text trigger until clicked.
2
Visual HierarchyA/B test the UI to ensure product imagery remains the dominant focus over the logistics tools.
Risk 03 · Behavioural
The "Artificial Urgency"
Backlash
▲ Medium–High Impact
Why it might fail
The 15-minute lock-in timer might feel like a manipulative dark pattern. Gen Z users are highly sensitive to forced urgency and may abandon the bag out of spite.
Guardrail
Bag Abandonment Rate (Post-Date Lock)
Mitigation Plan
1
Logistics-Framed Copy"To secure this slot with our courier, move to bag in 15 mins." (Never use "Hurry! Buy now!").
2
Soft ExpirationIf timer lapses, simply say "Slot expired — select a new date." No pressure, no penalties.
Risk 04 · Technical
API / Technical
Latency
⚠ High Impact
Why it might fail
Calculating dates via real-time inventory and pincodes adds lag. Every 100ms of delay in e-commerce drastically increases bounce rates.
Guardrail
API Latency (<300ms p95 Target)
Mitigation Plan
1
Async LoadingRender Wishlist instantly. Fetch dates asynchronously using skeleton loaders.
2
Edge CachingCache delivery timeframes for regional hubs to avoid complex supply chain queries per item.
Risk-Aware Rollout
Each risk has a linked guardrail metric — if any threshold is breached post-launch, rollout pauses and the corresponding mitigation is activated.
