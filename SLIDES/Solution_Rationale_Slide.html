Solution Rationale
"Need it By?" — Turning Passive Wishlisting Into Active Delivery Commitment
Target Segment: 68.8% Trust-Gated Shoppers
Psychological Lever: Commitment Bias (Zero discount needed)
From Problem To Solution
The Problem (H2)
How might we help high-intent Myntra wishlist users confidently commit to a purchase by surfacing delivery certainty at the moment of evaluation — before they reach checkout?
The Solution
Let the user name their date on the wishlist card. The system confirms or denies. Anxiety resolved before checkout.
Design Principles
P1: Passive to Active
Wishlist is a passive bookmark today. This feature makes it an active scheduling tool — the user declares intent, the platform commits.
P2: Surface Anxiety Pre-Checkout
Delivery date is hidden until checkout. We surface it at the wishlist stage — where the actual decision is made.
P3: Micro-Commitment
Locking in a delivery date creates urgency and confidence, naturally triggering the "Move to Bag" action.
How the feature works - 4 step flow
1
“Need it By?” on Wishlist CardInjected above 'Move to Bag' — intercepts hesitation at the decision point.
2
Date Selector ModalUser picks their hard deadline (dates).
3
Guaranteed Date ConfirmedSystem checks stock + logistics → Delivery on [Date] — locked in.
4
15-min Urgency HookMove to Bag in 15 mins to secure a slot. Scarcity — no coupon.
