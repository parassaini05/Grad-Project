## 1. Executive Summary: The Wishlist as a Revenue Lever
To sustainably increase Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) without eroding margins, Myntra must target the massive pool of high-intent users stuck in the wishlist phase. 

Myntra operates at a massive scale, with over **60 Million Weekly Active Users**. The platform generated over **₹6,043 Crore in revenue** in FY25 (+18% YoY), driven by three core streams: 
*   **Logistics (48.3%)** — ₹2,919 Cr
*   **Marketplace commissions (34%)** — ₹2,052 Cr
*   **Advertising (18%)** — ₹915 Cr

Converting a wishlist item is the single highest ROI action on the platform because of a **Triple Impact**: one conversion triggers all three revenue streams simultaneously. Furthermore, because the user has already self-selected the item, the Customer Acquisition Cost (**Zero CAC**) for that transaction is effectively nil.

To track this, our **North Star Metric** is the *30-Day Wishlist-to-Purchase Conversion Rate*. Currently, India's overall fashion CVR sits at just **1.2%–2%**. Wishlist users inherently possess much higher intent, yet their conversion remains deeply underoptimised.

## 2. The Mathematical User Journey
To understand where revenue is leaking, we map the metrics directly to the user journey:

*   **Top-Level Revenue:** `Total GMV = Total Orders × Average Order Value`
*   **Order Composition:** `Total Orders = Direct Orders + Wishlist Orders`
*   **The Wishlist Funnel:** `Wishlist Orders = Total Items Wishlisted × Wishlist-to-Purchase Conversion Rate`

By decomposing the conversion rate, the behavioral bottleneck becomes mathematically visible:
> `Conversion Rate = Wishlist View Rate × Wishlist Add-to-Bag Rate × Checkout Completion Rate`

## 3. Identifying the Core Bottleneck (The 3-Stage Funnel)
When applying Myntra user data to the funnel above, the break in the chain is strictly isolated to the middle stage (The Add-to-Bag decision):

1.  **Top of Funnel (Wishlist View Rate) is healthy:** Users return to view their wishlists on average **3.2 times** before purchasing. They are highly engaged. This is *not* the constraint.
2.  **Bottom of Funnel (Checkout Completion Rate) is healthy:** Once an item makes it into the bag, **~75% complete the purchase**. Users generally follow through on high commitment.
3.  **Middle of Funnel (Wishlist Add-to-Bag Rate) is broken:** Between **70% to 80%** of shoppers abandon the flow right at the 'Move to Bag' decision point due to delivery uncertainty and fit anxiety.

## 4. Quantitative Evidence & Market Data
Why does Stage 2 break? Users utilize the wishlist as a deliberation tool—**73% are actively comparing item attributes**, waiting for validation. We also see a **300% demand spike** during sales, proving that the demand exists; the barrier is trust and timing, not a lack of interest.

The data proves that the blockage is driven by operational trust issues, not price sensitivity:
*   **Fit & Size Anxiety (~30%):** A uniquely high barrier in fashion e-commerce.
*   **Delivery & Trust Anxiety (~24%):** Evidenced by high COD return rates, reflecting low confidence at purchase time.
*   **The Trust Solution (+25%):** Myntra data proves that integrating trust signals works, showing a **+25% CVR** lift with social commerce features.

## 5. Strategic Conclusion
The opportunity is strictly defined: Surface delivery certainty *before* the user clicks 'Move to Bag' to convert passive wishlisting into active commitment. 
**Constraint:** Must use operational transparency, not monetary discounting.
