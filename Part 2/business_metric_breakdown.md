# Part 2: Business Metric Breakdown

## 1. Top-Line Business Context: How Myntra Drives Revenue
Myntra’s core revenue model is transaction-based. To drive top-line revenue growth, the company must either acquire new users (high acquisition cost) or extract greater value from existing users. 

The wishlist represents **high-intent demand already present on the platform**. Millions of users browse fashion products and explicitly save items, yet a large proportion never convert. 

By unblocking this stagnant demand, Myntra achieves three core business objectives:
1. **Increase Purchase Frequency:** Converting dormant intent into active transactions shortens the gap between purchases.
2. **Improve Monetization:** Driving higher Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) from the existing user base without spending additional marketing dollars.
3. **Extract Value:** Turning a passive "bookmark" into a direct revenue-generating event.

## 2. The Strategic Metric
To realize these overarching business objectives, the growth team is targeting a specific leading indicator:

**Strategic Goal:** Increase the percentage of users who purchase at least one item from their wishlist within 30 days of adding it.

**Formula:** 
`Wishlist-to-Purchase Conversion Rate = (Users purchasing ≥1 wishlisted item within 30 days) / (Total users adding items to wishlist within 30 days)`

---

## Decomposition: Product Outcomes & User Behaviors

To move the macro business metric, we must break it down into leading product outcomes and specific user behaviors. 

### 1. Wishlist Return & Evaluation (Top of Funnel)
* **User Behavior:** The user revisits the wishlist to re-evaluate the saved items.
* **Product Outcome:** The wishlist provides enough actionable information (e.g., stock urgency, delivery confidence) to compel a decision rather than just acting as a static bookmark folder.
* **AI Discovery Engine Link:** Our engine revealed that users often use the wishlist as a "holding area" (Trust-Gated Shoppers) while they decide if they trust the platform enough to execute the purchase. They are actively evaluating *risk*, not just the product.

### 2. Add-to-Cart from Wishlist (Middle of Funnel)
* **User Behavior:** The user transitions the item from the wishlist to the active shopping cart.
* **Product Outcome:** The user's post-purchase anxiety (specifically regarding delivery timelines and return reliability) is sufficiently mitigated on the wishlist or product page to justify intent to buy.
* **AI Discovery Engine Link:** 59.7% of reviews cited "Trust Deficit" as the primary decision driver. Users hesitate to move items to the cart because of fear of cancellations, delayed delivery, and complicated returns.

### 3. Checkout Completion (Bottom of Funnel)
* **User Behavior:** The user completes the transaction without abandoning the cart.
* **Product Outcome:** The checkout process reinforces the guarantees and transparency promised earlier in the funnel, ensuring no last-minute friction.
* **AI Discovery Engine Link:** Only 3.2% of the friction was traditional cart abandonment. The major drop-off happens *before* the cart, driven by delivery and return anxiety (together accounting for over 60% of behavioral evidence).

---

## Where the Highest-Potential Opportunities Lie

By combining the metric decomposition with our AI Discovery Engine findings, the constraint that **monetary incentives cannot be used** becomes an advantage. We do not need to discount items to drive conversion; we need to eliminate operational anxiety.

### Opportunity Area 1: Rebuilding Operational Trust at the Wishlist Level (Highest Potential)
* **Why it matters:** 68.8% of users are "Trust-Gated Shoppers". They have high intent but pause due to a trust deficit.
* **What needs to change:** We must shift the user behavior from "waiting to see if I can trust Myntra" to "buying with confidence". This requires the product outcome of **radical transparency** regarding delivery success rates, return ease, and refund speed directly on the wishlist and product pages.

### Opportunity Area 2: Eliminating Delivery Anxiety Before Checkout
* **Why it matters:** Delivery complaints made up 54.3% of the evidence types. Users postpone purchases because they cannot see clear, reliable delivery timelines. 
* **What needs to change:** Users need proactive communication and guarantees about delivery timelines *before* they purchase. If we can increase certainty around the post-purchase experience while the item is still in the wishlist, the conversion to cart will naturally improve.

### Conclusion
The most impactful lever for improving the 30-day wishlist-to-purchase conversion is not changing the discovery of fashion, but rather **solving the post-purchase anxiety pre-purchase**. By focusing on the "Trust Deficit", we can unlock the pent-up demand of Trust-Gated Shoppers.
