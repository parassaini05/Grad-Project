## 2. Visual Layout Options
Since this is a visual presentation, don't use a wall of text. Use a split layout:

### Left Side: The Business Case & Metric Tree (The "What")
Start by connecting the feature to Myntra's overarching revenue goals.
* **Top-Line Impact:** Unlocking stagnant wishlist demand directly increases **Purchase Frequency** and **ARPU** (Average Revenue Per User) without relying on new acquisition costs.
* **Macro Metric:** 30-Day Wishlist-to-Purchase Conversion
  * ↳ Wishlist View Rate
  * ↳ **Wishlist-to-Bag Rate (🚨 The Bottleneck)**
  * ↳ Bag-to-Purchase Rate

### Right Side: The Behaviors & Outcomes (The "Why" & "How")
Map the bottleneck to the AI insights and the product outcome you need to achieve without using monetary incentives.
* **The User Behavior:** Users (68.8% Trust-Gated) use the Wishlist as a "holding area" while evaluating operational risk.
* **The AI Insight:** 59.7% of friction here is caused by a "Trust Deficit" (fear of bad delivery/returns).
* **The Product Outcome Needed:** We must inject *Radical Operational Transparency* (delivery speed, return success guarantees) directly onto the Wishlist UI to unblock the path to the Cart.

* **Font Size Constraint:** Ensure your body text is at least size 14 (Google Slides/PPT), 26 (Figma), or 22 (Canva).
* **Color Blindness:** Since you'll likely use red/green to highlight the bottleneck (Wishlist-to-Bag) vs. successful steps, use icons (like a 🚨 or ❌) alongside colors to ensure it's readable for color-blind evaluators.
