# Business Metric Tree: Wishlist → Purchase Conversion

To effectively influence the macro business metric, we must decompose it into a mathematical metric tree. By multiplying these conversion stages together, we arrive at our final 30-day conversion rate.

## The Mathematical Breakdown

**Wishlist → Purchase Conversion (30 Days)** = 
> `[Wishlist View Rate]` × `[Wishlist-to-Bag Rate]` × `[Bag-to-Purchase Rate]`

---

## 1. Wishlist View Rate (Top of Funnel)
**Definition:** The percentage of users who return to view their wishlist within 30 days of adding an item.
* **User Behavior:** Opening the app, clicking the 'WISHLIST' heart icon in the top navigation.
* **Product Outcome:** The wishlist must function as an active "decision board" rather than a passive "graveyard" for forgotten items.
* **AI Discovery Engine Context:** Our analysis shows users are actively using the wishlist as a "holding area" while they wait for external validation (YouTube hauls, Reddit reviews) to mitigate their operational risks.

## 2. Wishlist-to-Bag Rate (Middle of Funnel) — 🚨 THE BOTTLENECK
**Definition:** The percentage of wishlist views that result in a user clicking 'MOVE TO BAG' and successfully selecting a size.
* **User Behavior:** Overcoming hesitation, trusting the platform, selecting a size, and transitioning the item to the active cart.
* **Product Outcome:** The user's anxiety regarding post-purchase operations (delivery timelines, return friction, refund reliability) is sufficiently reduced on the wishlist page itself.
* **AI Discovery Engine Context:** This is where the funnel breaks. 
  * **68.8%** of users in our dataset are "Trust-Gated Shoppers".
  * **59.7%** of the friction here is caused by a general "Trust Deficit".
  * **54.3%** of complaints are specific to "Delivery Anxiety".
  * **Conclusion:** Users are not moving items to the bag because they do not trust what happens *after* they pay.

## 3. Bag-to-Purchase Rate (Bottom of Funnel)
**Definition:** The percentage of users who click 'PLACE ORDER' after moving a wishlisted item to their bag.
* **User Behavior:** Reviewing the final price, entering shipping details, and completing payment without clicking 'MOVE TO WISHLIST' to reverse the flow.
* **Product Outcome:** A seamless, transparent checkout experience with no hidden charges.
* **AI Discovery Engine Context:** Our AI engine found that traditional cart abandonment accounts for only **3.2%** of the overall friction. Once users overcome the trust deficit and add the item to the bag, they generally follow through.

---

## Conclusion: Targeting the Highest-Potential Opportunity

Since we are constrained by the rule that **we CANNOT offer monetary incentives (discounts)**, we cannot rely on artificial urgency to force the `Wishlist-to-Bag Rate`. 

Instead, we must solve the **Root Cause**.

The metric decomposition proves that the `Wishlist-to-Bag Rate` is the weakest link, and the AI Discovery Engine proves that this weakness is caused by **Operational Anxiety**.

**The Strategic Opportunity:**
To increase the macro business metric, we must introduce a product outcome that creates **radical operational transparency directly on the Wishlist UI**. If a user can see guaranteed delivery dates, return success rates, and fabric-quality social proof *while* evaluating their wishlist, we will unblock the 68.8% of Trust-Gated Shoppers and drastically improve the `Wishlist-to-Bag Rate`.
